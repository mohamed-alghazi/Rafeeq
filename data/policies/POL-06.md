# POL-06 — Synthetic refund rules

Madar is fictional. Fees are in SAR. A rejected application is eligible for its recorded fee minus 100 SAR, with a zero floor. Withdrawal before review returns the full recorded fee. Withdrawal during review returns half. A recorded duplicate payment is refundable up to the duplicate amount.

The assistant may prepare a refund request, with explicit human approval before creation. Requests above 1000 SAR must wait for a finance officer. This prototype records approved requests only; it does not connect to payment systems or pay money.

Source: SDAIA Academy, Agentic AI Systems Engineering, synthetic Madar POL-06. This is a concise project summary, not municipal guidance.
